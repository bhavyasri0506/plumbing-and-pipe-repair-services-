Pipe Repair & Replacement

Professional Pipe Repair Services

Pipe problems range from a slow drip behind a wall to a burst main flooding your home. AquaFix handles them all. We use non-invasive leak detection to pinpoint the source without breaking walls, then repair or replace with code-compliant materials.

Every job ends with a pressure test and a written warranty. We work with copper, PEX, PVC, CPVC, and galvanized pipe. Serving Hyderabad, Warangal, Vijayawada, and Visakhapatnam.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about pipe repair and replacement.

How much does pipe repair & replacement cost?

Our flat-rate pricing means you get a written quote before any work begins. Minor repairs start at ₹999, full repipes are quoted per linear foot. Your technician confirms the exact price on site.

Do you offer a warranty on pipe repairs?

Yes. All pipe repairs come with a written workmanship warranty — up to 10 years for full repipe installations.

How quickly can you come for a pipe emergency?

For standard bookings, we usually arrive the same day or next day. For burst pipe emergencies, our average arrival time is 45 minutes across Hyderabad.

Which pipe materials do you use?

We use premium PEX, copper, and CPVC depending on the application. All materials are ISI-certified and code-compliant.
