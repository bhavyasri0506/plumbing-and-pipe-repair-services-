Fixture Installation

Professional Fixture Installation Services

Upgrading your kitchen or bathroom fixtures? AquaFix supplies, installs, and tests every type of plumbing fixture. From a single faucet swap to a full set of kitchen and bath fixtures, we handle supply lines, drain assemblies, wax rings, and leak testing.

All installations come with a written warranty and clean installation — boot covers, drop cloths, and a spotless finish. Serving homeowners and businesses across Hyderabad, Warangal, Vijayawada, and Visakhapatnam.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about fixture installation.

How much does fixture installation cost?

Single fixture installations start at ₹699. Multi-fixture packages are discounted. Your technician provides a flat-rate quote before starting.

Can you install fixtures I already purchased?

Yes. We install customer-supplied fixtures provided they are compatible with your existing plumbing. We will advise on compatibility before starting.

Do you remove old fixtures?

Yes. Removal and eco-friendly disposal of old fixtures is included in the installation price. We leave your space clean and ready.

What brands do you recommend?

We recommend Jaquar, Kohler, Grohe, and Hindware for their quality and warranty support. We supply these at trade pricing.
