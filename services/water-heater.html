FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about water heaters.

How much does water heater installation cost?

Standard geyser installation starts at ₹2,499. Tankless systems and multi-point installations are quoted on site. The quote includes all materials and labour.

Tankless vs tank — which is better?

Tankless heaters save energy and provide endless hot water, but cost more upfront. Tank heaters are cheaper but less efficient. We help you choose based on your usage and budget.

How long do water heaters last?

A quality tank heater lasts 8–12 years. Tankless heaters last 15–20 years. Annual servicing extends the life of either type significantly.

Do you service existing water heaters?

Yes. We repair and service all brands and types of water heaters. A ₹699 service visit includes flushing, element check, and thermostat calibration.
