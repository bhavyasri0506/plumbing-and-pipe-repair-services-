FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about emergency plumbing.

How quickly can you come for an emergency?

Our average emergency response time is 45 minutes across Hyderabad. Call +91 80055 50199 and we dispatch the nearest available master plumber immediately.

Do you charge extra for emergency calls?

Emergency visits have a ₹499 call-out fee that is waived if you proceed with the repair. Home Care Plan members pay no call-out fee, even for emergencies.

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Burst pipes, major leaks, sewage backups, overflowing toilets, and complete loss of water are emergencies. If you are unsure, call us — we will advise.

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Our emergency line is answered by a live dispatcher 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. A licensed plumber is always on call.
