Drain Cleaning

Professional Drain Cleaning Services

A clogged drain is more than an inconvenience — it can cause water damage, bad odours, and health hazards. AquaFix uses high-pressure hydro-jetting, motorised augers, and video camera inspection to clear blockages and identify underlying issues.

We do not just poke a hole in the clog; we clear the line completely. Eco-friendly methods, no harsh chemicals — safe for your pipes and the environment. Serving Hyderabad, Warangal, Vijayawada, and Visakhapatnam.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about drain cleaning.

How much does drain cleaning cost?

Flat-rate pricing starts at ₹1,299 for a standard drain cleaning. Hydro-jetting and camera inspection are quoted on site based on the severity of the blockage.

Is hydro-jetting safe for my pipes?

Yes. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water that clears blockages without damaging pipes. It is safe for all standard residential plumbing.

How often should I clean my drains?

For most homes, an annual preventive cleaning keeps drains flowing. Kitchen drains may need cleaning every 6 months if you cook frequently.

Can you fix a completely blocked drain?

Yes. We use motorized augers and hydro-jetting to clear even the toughest blockages, including tree root intrusion in sewer lines.
