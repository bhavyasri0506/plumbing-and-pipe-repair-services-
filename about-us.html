Our story

From one van to Hyderabad's most trusted plumber

In 2011, Arjun Reddy started AquaFix with one van and a simple belief: plumbing service in Hyderabad deserved to be honest, punctual, and guaranteed.

Today, AquaFix has 12 service vehicles, 24 licensed plumbers, and serves thousands of homes — but the core never changed: upfront pricing, on-time arrival, and a written guarantee on every job.

Our Mission

To deliver plumbing so reliable and transparent that Hyderabad homeowners never dread calling a plumber. We earn trust one job at a time — with upfront pricing and master craftsmanship.

Our Vision

To be the plumbing company Hyderabad recommends without hesitation — the name neighbours pass to neighbours, and the standard every other plumber is measured against.
