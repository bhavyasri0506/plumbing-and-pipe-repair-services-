Our Story

From a One-Man Shop to a City-Wide Service

AquaFix began in 2009 with a single van, a toolbox, and a simple promise: treat every home like it's our own. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a team of 25 certified plumbers serving the entire metropolitan area.

Through every year, every job, and every customer, we've stayed true to our founding principle — quality work at honest prices. That's not just a slogan; it's the reason over 10,000 customers trust us with their plumbing.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted plumbing service in the region, known for quality, integrity, and innovation.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional plumbing solutions that protect homes, conserve water, and exceed expectations.
