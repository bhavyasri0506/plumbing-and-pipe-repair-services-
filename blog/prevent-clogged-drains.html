5 Simple Ways to Prevent Clogged Drains

Keep your drains flowing freely with these easy, everyday habits that prevent clogs before they start.

Kavya Rao
Project Manager
28 June 2026
6 min read

Drain clogs are the most common plumbing problem in Hyderabad homes — and almost all of them are preventable. Here are five simple habits that keep your drains flowing freely.

Why drains clog

Drains clog slowly, not suddenly. Hair, soap scum, cooking grease, and food particles build up over weeks and months until water can no longer pass. By the time you notice a slow drain, the clog is already significant.

1. Use a mesh strainer

A ₹400 mesh strainer catches hair, food bits, and soap scum before they enter your pipes. Clean it out after every shower and you will be amazed at how much gunk never makes it into your drain.

2. Pour hot water weekly

Once a week, fill your sink with hot water (not boiling — that can crack porcelain), then pull the plug. The volume of hot water helps flush any buildup before it hardens. It takes 30 seconds and saves you a ₹2,000 service call.

3. Avoid chemical drain cleaners

Those bottles of liquid drain opener do not really clear clogs — they burn a small hole through them. Worse, the lye and acid eat your pipes over time. Use a plunger or a ₹1,600 drain snake instead. For stubborn clogs, call for a hydro-jet.

4. Dispose of grease properly

Cooking oil and grease are the #1 cause of kitchen drain clogs. Never pour grease down the sink — even with hot water. Let it cool, wipe it into a tissue, and bin it. Your pipes will thank you.

5. Schedule annual cleaning

Even with the best habits, drains accumulate buildup over time. A professional hydro-jet cleaning once a year keeps your pipes like new — and costs far less than an emergency call when a clog finally blocks the line completely.

Bottom line

Prevention is always cheaper than repair. These five habits take less than five minutes a week and can save you thousands of rupees in plumbing bills.
