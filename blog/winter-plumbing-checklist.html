Winter Plumbing Checklist: Protect Your Pipes This Season

Simple steps to prevent frozen pipes, geyser failures, and winter water damage in your Hyderabad home.

Kavya Rao
Project Manager
28 June 2026
6 min read

Winter in Hyderabad may not bring snow, but the temperature drop from November to February can still stress your plumbing. Cold mornings can cause pipes to contract, geysers to work harder, and hidden leaks to worsen. Here is your winter plumbing checklist.

Why winter is tough on plumbing

Even in Hyderabad, winter temperatures can drop to 10°C or lower. This causes metal pipes to contract, joints to loosen, and water heaters to work overtime. The combination can lead to leaks, bursts, and geyser failures — usually at the most inconvenient time.

1. Insulate exposed pipes

Pipes in exterior walls, lofts, or outside are most vulnerable. Wrap them with foam pipe insulation — it costs just ₹50 per metre and takes minutes to install. This prevents both heat loss from hot water pipes and condensation on cold water pipes.

2. Service your geyser

Before winter hits, have your geyser serviced. A technician will flush the tank to remove sediment, check the heating element, test the thermostat, and inspect the pressure relief valve. A ₹699 service visit can prevent a ₹15,999 emergency replacement.

3. Disconnect outdoor hoses

If you have a garden hose connected to an outdoor tap, disconnect it for winter. Water left in the hose can freeze and damage both the hose and the tap. Drain the hose and store it indoors.

4. Keep cabinets open

On very cold nights, open the cabinet doors under your kitchen and bathroom sinks to let warm air circulate around the pipes. This simple step prevents the pipes inside from getting too cold.

5. Know your shut-off valve

If a pipe does burst, you need to shut off the water fast. Find your main shut-off valve now — before an emergency — and make sure everyone in the household knows where it is. Every minute saved is litres of water that does not flood your home.

Bottom line

A little preparation goes a long way. Spend ₹500 on insulation and a geyser service now, and you could save ₹50,000 in emergency repairs this winter.
