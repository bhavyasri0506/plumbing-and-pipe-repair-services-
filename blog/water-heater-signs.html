7 Signs Your Water Heater Is About to Fail

Catch these warning signs early and avoid a costly flood. Your water heater is telling you something — learn to listen.

Kavya Rao
Project Manager
28 June 2026
6 min read

A failing water heater rarely fails silently. It sends warnings for weeks — sometimes months — before it quits completely. The trick is knowing what those warnings look like, because a ₹1,600 part replaced today can save you a ₹2,50,000 flood tomorrow.

1. Rusty or discoloured water

If your hot water comes out rusty or brown, the inside of your tank is corroding. This is the #1 sign your heater is near the end of its life. Once the tank starts rusting from the inside, it is only a matter of time before it leaks.

2. Strange noises

Rumbling, popping, or banging sounds mean sediment has built up at the bottom of the tank. The sediment traps water under it, which boils and bursts through, creating the noise. A flush can help, but persistent noise means the tank is failing.

3. Water takes too long to heat

If your geyser used to heat water in 10 minutes and now takes 30, the heating element is failing or the tank is so full of sediment that there is no room for water. Either way, it is time to call a plumber.

4. Leaking tank

Any water around the base of your water heater is a red flag. It could be a loose fitting — or it could be a crack in the tank. A cracked tank cannot be repaired; it must be replaced before it floods your home.

5. Low hot water pressure

If only your hot water has low pressure, sediment or scale is blocking the outlet pipe. This is common in older heaters and often means the tank is near the end of its life.

6. Higher electricity bills

A failing heater works harder to heat the same amount of water. If your electricity bill has crept up with no other explanation, your geyser could be the culprit.

7. Age over 10 years

Tank water heaters last 8–12 years. If yours is over 10, do not wait for a failure — plan a replacement on your schedule, not the heater's.

Bottom line

Catching these signs early turns a ₹1,24,000 emergency into a ₹99,500 planned replacement — and saves you the cleanup. Not sure what to look for? We will inspect your heater free with any service call.
