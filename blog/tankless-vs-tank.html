Tankless vs Tank Water Heaters: Which Is Right for You?

A clear comparison of upfront cost, efficiency, and lifespan to help you choose the right water heater for your home.

Kavya Rao
Project Manager
28 June 2026
6 min read

Choosing between a tank and a tankless water heater is one of the biggest plumbing decisions a Hyderabad homeowner makes. Here is a clear, no-nonsense comparison.

Upfront cost

Tank water heaters win here, easily. A 25-litre tank installed runs ₹2,499–₹4,999. A tankless system, once you factor in gas line upgrades, electrical work, and venting, runs ₹8,999–₹15,999. That is a real gap — and it is the main reason most homes still have tanks.

Energy efficiency

Tankless heaters are about 25–35% more efficient because they do not keep 25 litres of water hot all day. For a typical family, that is ₹8,300–₹12,450 a year. At that rate, it takes 8–14 years to break even on the upfront difference — which is right around the lifespan of a tankless unit.

Lifespan

Tank heaters last 8–12 years. Tankless units last 15–20 years — nearly double. If you plan to stay in your home long-term, the longer lifespan narrows the cost gap considerably.

Hot water capacity

Tank heaters can run out of hot water if everyone showers at once. Tankless heaters provide endless hot water — but only one tap at a time unless you install a high-capacity unit. For large families, a tank or a high-flow tankless is the better choice.

Which should you choose?

Choose a tank if: You want the lowest upfront cost, you have limited wall space, or your electrical panel cannot handle a tankless unit.

Choose tankless if: You want endless hot water, you are renovating (the wall space is already open), or you plan to stay in your home for 10+ years.
