Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. Our emergency line is answered by a live dispatcher 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. A licensed technician is typically dispatched within 45 minutes of your call. Call +91 80055 50199 anytime.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Every technician on our team is a licensed journeyman or master plumber, fully background-checked, and covered by ₹2 crore liability insurance plus workers comp. We are happy to provide license numbers and proof of insurance before any work begins.

How much will my repair cost?

We use flat-rate upfront pricing. After diagnosing the issue, your technician provides a written quote before any work begins. There are no hourly charges and no hidden fees — the price you approve is the price you pay. See our pricing page for typical ranges.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. All repairs are backed by a written workmanship warranty of up to 10 years, depending on the service. If something fails due to our work within the warranty period, we fix it at no charge — no questions asked.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve the greater Hyderabad metro area, including Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli, Madhapur, Kukatpally, and Secunderabad. Call us to confirm coverage for your neighborhood.

Can you help with insurance claims?

Absolutely. For emergency water damage situations, we provide detailed documentation, photos, and invoices to support your insurance claim. We have helped thousands of customers navigate the claims process.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

Standard visits have no call-out fee. Emergency visits have a ₹499 call-out fee that is waived if you proceed with the recommended repair. Home Care Plan members pay no call-out fee ever.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Jobs over ₹12,000 qualify for 0% APR financing over 6 or 12 months, subject to credit approval. Ask your technician for details — we can usually get you approved in minutes.

How do I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Call us at +91 80055 50199 at least 2 hours before your scheduled window. There is no fee to reschedule. Same-day cancellations within 2 hours may incur a ₹499 trip fee if the technician is already en route.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), debit cards, UPI, bank transfer, Apple Pay, and cash. Personal checks are accepted from established customers only.
