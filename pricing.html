What is included

  • On-site inspection and diagnosis
  • All standard materials and fittings
  • Labour and installation
  • Leak test and pressure test
  • Workmanship warranty
  • Clean-up of the work area

What is charged separately

  • Premium fixture brands (Jaquar, Grohe, Kohler)
  • Drywall, tile, or flooring repair
  • Concealed piping behind finished walls
  • Sewer jetting beyond the main line
  • GST (18%) on the total invoice
