Drain Cleaning

Professional Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

Slow drains and clogs are more than just an inconvenience — they can lead to bigger plumbing problems if left untreated. At AquaFix, we use advanced drain cleaning technology including hydro-jetting and camera inspection to clear blockages and keep your plumbing system flowing smoothly.

Our drain cleaning service doesn't just clear the immediate clog — we identify and address the root cause to prevent future issues. From kitchen sinks to main sewer lines, no job is too big or too small for our experienced team.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about drain cleaning.

How often should I get my drains cleaned?

We recommend professional drain cleaning once a year as preventive maintenance. If you experience frequent clogs, more frequent cleaning may be necessary.

Is hydro-jetting safe for my pipes?

Yes, hydro-jetting is safe for most pipes. Our technicians assess your pipe condition first and adjust water pressure accordingly to prevent any damage.

What causes recurring drain clogs?

Common causes include grease buildup, tree root intrusion, and pipe misalignment. Our camera inspection identifies the exact cause so we can fix it permanently.
