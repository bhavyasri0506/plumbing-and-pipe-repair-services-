5 Signs You Need Pipe Repair Before It's Too Late

Hidden leaks, discolored water, low pressure — here are 5 warning signs you need pipe repair before it becomes a costly emergency.

Robert Hayes
Senior Plumber
15 January 2026
5 min read

As a homeowner, your plumbing system is one of the most critical — and most easily overlooked — parts of your property. Pipes hidden behind walls and under floors can develop problems long before you notice them. But if you know what to look for, you can catch issues early and save thousands in water damage repairs.

Why early detection matters

In this article, we'll walk you through the five most common warning signs that your pipes need professional attention. Don't ignore these red flags — acting quickly can mean the difference between a simple repair and a major renovation.

1. Unexplained increase in water bills

If your water bill has suddenly spiked and your usage habits haven't changed, you may have a hidden leak. Even a small pipe leak can waste hundreds of gallons of water per month. Compare your recent bills to the same period last year — a significant difference is a red flag that warrants a professional inspection.

2. Discolored or rusty water

When you turn on the tap and see brown, yellow, or discolored water, it often means your pipes are corroding from the inside. This is especially common in older homes with galvanized steel pipes. Corroded pipes don't just affect water quality — they're also at high risk of leaking and bursting. If you notice discolored water, have your pipes inspected immediately.

3. Low water pressure

A sudden drop in water pressure can indicate a blockage, a leak, or pipe damage. If only one fixture is affected, the problem may be localized. But if the pressure drop is throughout your home, it could point to a more serious issue in your main water line. Don't ignore persistent low pressure — it rarely fixes itself.

4. Strange sounds from your pipes

Pipes that bang, gurgle, or whine are trying to tell you something. These sounds can indicate air in the lines, water hammer effects, or pressure issues. While some noises are harmless, persistent sounds should be investigated by a professional plumber before they lead to bigger problems.

5. Visible water stains or mold

Water stains on ceilings or walls, damp spots, or unexplained mold growth are clear signs of a hidden leak. By the time these become visible, the leak has likely been present for some time. Don't wait — call a plumber immediately to prevent further structural damage and potential health risks from mold.

Bottom line

The average hidden pipe leak wastes 10,000 gallons of water per year — enough to fill a backyard swimming pool. Don't wait for visible damage. If you notice any of these signs, call AquaFix today.
